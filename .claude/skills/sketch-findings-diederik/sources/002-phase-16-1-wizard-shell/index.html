Step 2 of 6 · Welcome

A calmer view of your money

diederik pulls together your bank statements, credit-card statements, PayPal, and (if you want) your receipts from email — and shows you the real picture of where your money goes each month.

Everything stays on this computer. No cloud, no signup with us, no accountant looking over your shoulder.

What you'll need
  • Your bank login (mijn.asnbank.nl)
  • Your ICS Cards PDF statement
  • ~5 minutes
What we don't need
  • Your card numbers
  • Cloud access to your inbox
  • Anything stored online
