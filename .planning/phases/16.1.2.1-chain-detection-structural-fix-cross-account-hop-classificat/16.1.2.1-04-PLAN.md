<objective>
Wire BOTH consumers of the alias bridge: `ClassifyTransactionType::run()` step 2 AND `PairTransferCandidates::handle()` partner-account lookup. Both consult `Modules\Import\Public\Contracts\ResolvesKnownCounterpartyIban` (delivered by Plan 03) so the real-institution-IBAN → synthetic-IBAN-account bridge is used at BOTH places where the gap manifests.

Purpose: Plan 03 delivers the contract + the foundation (table, seeder, resolver). This plan wires the two existing call sites that need it:

1. `ClassifyTransactionType::run()` step 2 — currently only retypes when `counterparty_iban` literally equals one of the user's own `accounts.iban` values. After this plan, it consults `ResolvesKnownCounterpartyIban::resolveAccount(...)` FIRST, falling back to the literal-equality check.

2. `PairTransferCandidates::handle()` — currently does `where('iban', $tx->counterparty_iban)->first()` to find the partner account. After this plan, when that literal lookup returns null, it falls back to `ResolvesKnownCounterpartyIban::resolveAccount(...)`. Without this fallback (the checker's Blocker 2), even after Plan 02 (PayPal funding-leg → `transfer_in`) lands, the ASN-side `transfer_out` and the PayPal-side `transfer_in` cannot be paired because the ASN-side row's `counterparty_iban='LU89751000135104200E'` does not literally equal the user's synthetic `'PAYPAL'` IBAN.

Output: Both consumer files modified to inject and consult `ResolvesKnownCounterpartyIban` (via Public contract — cross-module boundary preserved). Pest unit coverage for both consult sites + an end-to-end feature test that proves the full chain (ASN expense → alias retype → ASN transfer_out, PayPal Bankstorting → Plan 02 transfer_in, PairTransferCandidates pairs the two via the alias-bridge fallback, `pair_transaction_id` bidirectional and non-null on both rows).
