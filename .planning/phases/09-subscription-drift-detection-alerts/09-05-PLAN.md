Wave 4 polish. Land the four remaining surfaces: CancellationImpactQuery (Phase 10 hand-off + inline "save €X/yr" display), per-series threshold override editor (popover on /drift + /recurring/series/{id}), global threshold field on /settings, and the hybrid snooze-revival mechanism (hourly scheduled job + query-time conditional). Purpose: Wave 3 ships a usable surface; Wave 4 makes it complete. Without the threshold override, volatile series produce alert noise; without the global threshold setting, users have no way to widen tolerance globally; without snooze revival, snoozed alerts never come back. Without CancellationImpactQuery's inline display, the alert is a complaint without a remedy. The Phase 10 hand-off (CancellationImpactQuery) is the load-bearing contract that lets Phase 10 build the rich what-if-cancel modal without re-deriving the math.

Output: A complete Phase 9 surface. After Wave 4, the user can: see drift alerts; act on them; customise threshold per-series and globally; rely on snoozed alerts re-surfacing automatically. Five new tests verify each Wave 4 capability.
