<objective>
Build the Tax module's read layer: `TaxYearQuery` (grouped per-year data with
year-override resolution and settled-EUR totals) and `TaxTagQuery` (the
lightweight tagged-state lookup the four badge surfaces, dashboard card, and
sidebar count consume). Extend `NavCountsService` with a `tax_tagged` key.

Purpose: Every downstream surface (/tax page, exporters, badge, dashboard,
nav) reads through these two services. Centralising the COALESCE year-override
logic here means the export and page never re-implement (and never diverge on)
the tax-year rule.
Output: Two query services producing the TaxYearData / TaxYearSummary DTOs and
scalar tagged-state maps; sidebar count wired.
</objective>
