<objective>
Ship the per-country deduction-category corpus (NL + DE + BE + FR + GB + US),
the `TaxCorpusLoader` that reads it, the `TaxCategoryWriter` that seeds and
manages user-owned categories with provenance-safe upserts, and the Settings
Tax section that exposes country choice + category CRUD (D-05..D-08).

per-country category set. Corpus-as-data (not code) means new countries ship
without code changes, and user renames are never clobbered by corpus updates.
Output: Corpus files; loader; category writer; Settings Tax section wired into
the settings page.
