# Legal

The two long-form pieces of context that NOTICE.md and the README link out to:
the licensing rationale and the data-handling contract.

## Topics

| File | What it covers |
|---|---|
| [license-rationale.md](license-rationale.md) | Why the project ships under the Hippocratic License 3.0 instead of an OSI-approved permissive license, and why it ships without paid code-signing certificates |
| [data-retention.md](data-retention.md) | What data beatrax stores, where it lives, how long it stays, and how a user removes it |

The authoritative license text lives at `LICENSE` in the repo root. The files in this
subtree explain the choices behind those documents in plain prose; they do not
substitute for the legal text.
